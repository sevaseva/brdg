## Reading the auction

- North opens **1♦** with 13 HCP and four cards in each minor. With 4–4 in the
  minors, the partnership agreement is to open **1♦**.
- South has 14 HCP, a balanced 4-3-3-3 hand, and no four-card major. South
  responds **2NT**, an invitational balanced bid.
- North accepts the invitation with **3NT**. North–South have **27 HCP**
  between them.
- South is **declarer**, not North: South was the first player on the
  North–South side to bid no-trumps. North's 3NT only raised the denomination
  South had already named.
- North–South do have an **eight-card club fit** (KJ54 opposite AQ32), but both
  hands are balanced, so no-trumps is the better place to play. Majors > NT >
  minors.
