What in the World Is Bridge?

_□×

Bridge is a card game for four players in two partnerships. You bid to decide who names the trump suit, then try to take tricks without accidentally giving away the store. It is like whist, but with more paperwork.

♠ Trump
The suit that beats everything else. Not a political statement.
♥ The dummy
One player's cards go face-up on the table. This is not a comment on their bidding. Usually.
♦ The contract
What you promised to make. See also: a binding agreement to disappoint your partner.
♣ The club
Also the place you are reading about. Confusing? Yes. Welcome to bridge.

Learn Bridge in Four Easy Bids

_□×
  1. 1

    Show up.

    This is the hardest part. The rest is cards.

  2. 2

    Learn that "1 club" does not mean "let's go to the club."

    It is a bid. The club is the building. The building is also a suit. Try to keep up.

  3. 3

    Take a trick by accident.

    Celebrate quietly. The experts can smell fear.

  4. 4

    Come back next time.

    Bring cookies. Become a regular. Win the cookie.

Bonus lesson: there is no shame in asking what just happened. There is only shame in asking the same question three Thursdays in a row. (We'll still answer.)
