★ Movie Night at the Club

_□×

The club has a projector, a folding screen, and strong opinions about popcorn.

This page is unrelated to bridge. No trump, no dummy, no partner to blame. It is here strictly for fun — and for picking a movie.

Propose a movie. Vote for the ones you would actually watch — as many as you like. Put a night on the calendar, then tell us if you can make it.

  1. Propose a movie: title, trailer link, and an optional one-liner.
  2. Vote for the ones you would watch.
  3. Pick a night with yes, maybe, or no.

No bridge. Just movies. The popcorn is a shared responsibility.

One name, one ballot. Vote for as many movies and nights as you like.
