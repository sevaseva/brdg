★ Standard American Yellow Card

_□×

We are learning Standard American Yellow Card, or SAYC, which is a long name for a sensible first bidding system. The "yellow card" is the summary sheet it is named after, not a warning label.

We take it one piece at a time: a session introduces an opening bid or a response, the next one builds on it, and nobody is expected to memorize a booklet over a single evening. This page is the reference shelf — the official booklets now, and the club's own notes as they appear.
